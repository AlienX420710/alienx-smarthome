## External TLS checks

SSL.org's certificate checker and vulnerability tester were used through a real
browser, with separate submissions for apex and www. Certificate checks disabled
redirect following. Both certificates were trusted, matched the requested host,
had a complete chain, and were valid August 26–November 25, 2026.

| Host                    | Certificate scan IP | Supported protocols | Vulnerability scan              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| alienxsmarthome.com     | 172.67.184.138      | TLS 1.2, TLS 1.3    | TLS 1.0/1.1 explicitly disabled |
| www.alienxsmarthome.com | 104.21.80.140       | TLS 1.2, TLS 1.3    | TLS 1.0/1.1 explicitly disabled |

Both vulnerability scans reached 104.21.80.140 and reported the same three
potential issues: BREACH (Brotli compression of `/`), LUCKY13 (CBC heuristic),
and Marvin (RSA key-exchange heuristic). These are scanner observations, not
confirmed exploits. The homepage is prerendered public content in this repository;
no per-user secret is rendered into that page, so compression alone does not
establish a BREACH vulnerability there. This assessment does not generalize to
future authenticated/dynamic pages. The CBC/RSA findings remain unresolved;
no timing study or Cloudflare cipher-policy change was performed.

The remaining tested vulnerability rows were OK; session resumption was informational.
The protocol portion of AX-016 has evidence. The broader cipher-policy portion
stays open. No blanket zero-vulnerability statement is justified.

Sources: [certificate checker](https://www.ssl.org/),
[vulnerability tester](https://www.ssl.org/ssl-vulnerability-test),
[Cloudflare cipher customization](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/cipher-suites/customize-cipher-suites/).
Cloudflare documents Advanced Certificate Manager as a requirement for custom
cipher suites. No subscription, purchase or current account entitlement is assumed.
